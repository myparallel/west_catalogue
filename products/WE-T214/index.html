WE-T214 3-in-1 Soil Sensor TransmitterWE-T214 三合一土壤传感器变送器

A rugged, field-ready transmitter for precision agriculture. Simultaneously monitors soil temperature, volumetric moisture content, and electrical conductivity (EC). Supports dual 4G and LoRa connectivity for reliable data backhaul in remote areas.一款坚固耐用的野外型变送器，适用于精准农业。同时监测土壤温度、体积含水率和电导率（EC），支持4G和LoRa双通信，确保偏远地区数据可靠回传。

Key Highlights产品亮点

Precision monitoring meets rugged industrial design.精准监测与工业级设计相结合。

3-in-1 Precise Sensing三合一精准传感

Monitors Soil Temperature, Volumetric Moisture Content, and Electrical Conductivity (EC) simultaneously with a high-accuracy triple-probe sensor.通过高精度三合一探针，同时监测土壤温度、体积含水率和电导率（EC）。

Dual 4G & LoRa4G与LoRa双通信

Flexible connectivity with 4G LTE (MQTT/HTTP) and LoRa (up to 5km range), ensuring data reporting even in extremely remote locations.灵活选择4G LTE（MQTT/HTTP）或LoRa（最远5km）通信方式，即使在偏远地区也能确保数据上报。

GNSS PositioningGNSS定位

Built-in GPS/GLONASS/BDS support for automated asset tracking and mapping of soil data across large plantations.内置GPS/GLONASS/BDS支持，自动追踪设备位置并在大型种植园中绘制土壤数据地图。

Ultra-long Endurance超长续航

Powered by a 19,000mAh high-capacity lithium battery or optional 40W solar panel for years of maintenance-free operation.采用19000mAh大容量锂电池或可选40W太阳能板供电，实现多年免维护运行。

Rugged & Reliable坚固可靠

IP67-rated host and IP68-rated probe enclosure. Engineered for harsh agricultural environments from -40°C to +85°C.主机IP67防护，探针IP68防护，专为-40°C至+85°C的严苛农业环境设计。

Real-time LCD Display实时LCD显示

On-site data viewing via a high-contrast display. View temperature, humidity, and EC levels directly without a mobile device.通过高对比度显示屏现场查看数据，无需移动设备即可直接读取温度、湿度和EC值。

Typical Applications典型应用场景
