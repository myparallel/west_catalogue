WE-T800 Multi-Function Field Monitor

A sealed IP68 field unit for distributed monitoring: 4G backhaul, RS485 Modbus-RTU, two 4–20 mA analog channels, two digital inputs, and flexible power from internal Li-ion packs or 9–30 V DC. Built for water utilities, environmental sites, and industrial IoT where reliability matters.

Key Highlights产品亮点

Precision monitoring meets rugged industrial design.精准监测与工业级设计相结合。

4G & Cloud-ready

LTE FDD/TDD bands supported; MQTT, HTTP, and WEST cloud protocols; 3-year cellular data plan option (100 MB/month).

Field I/O

1× RS485 (4800–115200 bps, Modbus-RTU), 2× AI (4–20 mA), 2× DI (switch or pulse counting), 2× configurable DC outputs (3.6 / 5 / 12 V).

Rugged & Readable

IP68 enclosure; 60×32 mm LCD with LED indicators and keypad for local readings and alarms.

Dual Power

Internal Li-ion options (114000 or 228000 mAh) with automatic handover to 9–30 V DC external supply.

Mobile Supervision

Bluetooth for local configuration; companion app for remote visibility.

Configurable Timing

Sampling and upload periods configurable from 10 s to 18 h to match battery life and network duty cycle.
