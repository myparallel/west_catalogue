WE-W200 Professional Weather Station

A professional-grade weather station for precision agriculture, meteorology, and environmental monitoring. Measures wind speed, wind direction, rainfall, temperature, humidity, barometric pressure, and solar radiation with high-accuracy sensors.

For technical support or customized OEM versions, email us from Contact Information.如需技术支持或定制OEM版本，请通过联系方式发送邮件。

Key Highlights产品亮点

Precision monitoring meets rugged industrial design.精准监测与工业级设计相结合。

7-in-1 Environmental Sensing

Wind speed, wind direction, rainfall, temperature, humidity, barometric pressure, and solar radiation all in a single compact station.

Dual 4G & LoRa

Connect via 4G LTE (MQTT/HTTP) or LoRa up to 5km range. Choose your preferred backhaul for any deployment location.

Solar Powered

Built-in 20W solar panel with 12,000mAh backup battery for true off-grid operation, 365 days a year.

Industrial Durability

IP67-rated enclosure with UV-resistant coating. Operates reliably from -40°C to +85°C in extreme environments.

GNSS Positioning

Integrated GPS/GLONASS/BDS for automatic geo-tagging of weather data across distributed sensor networks.

Cloud Platform

WEST Cloud dashboard with real-time charts, alerts via email/SMS, and API access for third-party integration.
