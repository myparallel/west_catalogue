WE-W200 Professional Weather StationWE-W200 专业气象站

A professional-grade weather station for precision agriculture, meteorology, and environmental monitoring. Measures wind speed, wind direction, rainfall, temperature, humidity, barometric pressure, and solar radiation with high-accuracy sensors.一款专业级气象站，适用于精准农业、气象和环境监测。采用高精度传感器测量风速、风向、雨量、温度、湿度、气压和太阳辐射。

Key Highlights产品亮点

Precision monitoring meets rugged industrial design.精准监测与工业级设计相结合。

7-in-1 Environmental Sensing七合一环境传感

Wind speed, wind direction, rainfall, temperature, humidity, barometric pressure, and solar radiation all in a single compact station.风速、风向、雨量、温度、湿度、气压和太阳辐射，全部集成在一台紧凑型气象站中。

Dual 4G & LoRa4G与LoRa双通信

Connect via 4G LTE (MQTT/HTTP) or LoRa up to 5km range. Choose your preferred backhaul for any deployment location.通过4G LTE（MQTT/HTTP）或LoRa（最远5km）连接，根据部署位置灵活选择通信方式。

Solar Powered太阳能供电

Built-in 20W solar panel with 12,000mAh backup battery for true off-grid operation, 365 days a year.内置20W太阳能板和12000mAh备用电池，真正实现离网运行，全年365天不间断。

Industrial Durability工业级耐久性

GNSS PositioningGNSS定位
