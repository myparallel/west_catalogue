WE-X280 Industrial Edge Computing GatewayWE-X280 边缘智慧网关

A powerful industrial edge computing gateway supporting LoRa wireless and RS485 wired convergence networks. Connects up to 128 wired/wireless devices, features 4G full Netcom and Ethernet dual-channel communication, edge computing engine, and seamless integration with the WEST-AIOT cloud platform.一款强大的工业级边缘计算网关，支持LoRa无线和RS485有线融合网络，最多接入128台有线/无线设备，具备4G全网通和以太网双通道通信，内置边缘计算引擎，可与WEST-AIOT云平台无缝对接。

Key Highlights产品亮点

Precision monitoring meets rugged industrial design.精准监测与工业级设计相结合。

Multi-Network Convergence多网融合

Supports 4G full Netcom, Ethernet, LoRa (410–490MHz), and RS485 wired networks simultaneously for flexible device connectivity.同时支持4G全网通、以太网、LoRa（410–490MHz）和RS485有线网络，实现灵活的设备连接。

Edge Computing Engine边缘计算引擎

Built-in edge computing capabilities for high-precision digital conversion, exception cleaning, protocol unification, and business logic calculation at the edge.内置边缘计算能力，在边缘端完成高精度数模转换、异常清洗、协议统一和业务逻辑计算。

Massive Device Access海量设备接入

One gateway connects up to 128 wired/wireless sensing devices, with expandable support for up to 16 I/O node modules.单台网关最多接入128台有线/无线传感设备，可扩展支持16个I/O节点模块。

Dual-Channel Communication双通道通信

4G full Netcom (LTE FDD/TDD) and Ethernet (RJ45, 10/100M) dual-channel encrypted transmission to the cloud platform.4G全网通（LTE FDD/TDD）和以太网（RJ45，10/100M）双通道加密传输至云平台。

Cloud Platform Integration云平台对接

Seamlessly connects to WEST-AIOT device management platform and third-party platforms via MQTT + custom JSON protocol.通过MQTT+自定义JSON协议，无缝对接WEST-AIOT设备管理平台和第三方平台。

Industrial Reliability工业级可靠性

Rail-mounted design, ABS/PC housing, wide temperature range (-20°C to +70°C), CE certified for demanding industrial environments.导轨式安装，ABS/PC外壳，宽温范围（-20°C至+70°C），CE认证，适应严苛工业环境。

Typical Applications典型应用场景

Optimized for diverse agricultural and environmental monitoring needs.适用于多种农业和环境监测需求。

Building Energy Management建筑能源管理

Monitor and optimize energy consumption in office buildings, commercial real estate, and factories. Connect various sensors via LoRa and RS485 for comprehensive energy monitoring and emission reduction.监控和优化办公楼、商业地产和工厂的能源消耗。通过LoRa和RS485连接各类传感器，实现全面的能源监测和节能减排。

Industrial IoT (IIoT)工业物联网

Bridge distributed field devices to cloud SCADA and MQTT/HTTP endpoints. The edge computing engine processes data locally before secure uplink to the WEST-AIOT platform.将分布式现场设备桥接到云SCADA和MQTT/HTTP终端。边缘计算引擎在数据安全上传到WEST-AIOT平台之前进行本地处理。
